Where I can help

The gap is rarely access.
It's judgment.

The common gap is not access to a coding agent. It is knowing what should be specified, what can be delegated, what must be reviewed, and how to tell whether generated work still matches the product and architecture. That's where most of an engagement's value sits.

Three ways to engage.

Each scoped to a clear deliverable
01 · Diagnostic

One-week drift audit

I review your AI-coded codebase, run an experimental import, and produce a written report: where you're drifting, what's load-bearing, what would stabilize it, and whether VibeLoom is the right fit.

Duration
5 working days
Deliverable
Written report + 90-min review call
What you walk away with
  • A named list of where intent and code have diverged, with item-ID anchors.
  • A candidate contract reconstructed from the existing code (brownfield import).
  • A go / no-go recommendation on adoption — and what would have to change for it to make sense.
02 · Adoption

Bootstrap & transfer

Hands-on engagement to stand up VibeLoom on your project. Pick a mode together, run the bootstrap, walk your team through the contract stack, and transfer ownership cleanly. Lasts as long as it needs to.

Duration
2-6 weeks typical
Deliverable
Live, governed contract + trained team
What you walk away with
  • An approved contract stack, mode-appropriate, in your repo.
  • A team that can run generate, review, reconcile, and approve without me in the room.
  • A first regeneration cycle and a first reconciliation, both observed, both documented.
03 · Bespoke

Custom governance design

For teams whose constraints don't fit the four standard modes — regulated environments, novel workflows, hybrid human/agent orgs. We design the governance flow together, then implement it as Skill extensions.

Duration
Project-scoped
Deliverable
Custom Skill + ops playbook
What you walk away with
  • A governance flow tailored to your approval policy, regulatory or organizational.
  • A custom Skill plus references that load on demand inside Claude Code or Codex.
  • An ops playbook for the human side: who reviews what, when, against which artifacts.

Engagements typically start in the low five figures. Exact scope priced after a 30-minute scoping call.
