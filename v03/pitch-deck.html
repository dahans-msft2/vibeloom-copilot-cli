06 / 10 Competition

Not Cursor's fight.

Cursor sells productivity to individual developers. We sell governance to engineering leaders. Different layer, different buyer, different metric.

Cursor / Copilot / Codeium Vibe coding — chat-driven, in the IDE. Per-edit velocity.
Kiro / Spec Kit / BMAD Spec-driven — per-feature specs. Decay between features.
VibeLoom Contract-driven — system-level contracts govern many cycles. Drift detected at the contract layer, not in code.

We are not in the same fight as Cursor. We win by being one abstraction level up.
